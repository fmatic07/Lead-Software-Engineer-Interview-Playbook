# Module 08 — Company Interview Playbooks

> Reverse-engineer how target companies evaluate Senior → Architect engineers. Connect Java, Spring Boot, System Design, Leadership, Behavioral, and Architecture prep to company-specific loops.

---

## How to Use This Module

1. Pick **1–2 named company playbooks** for active pipelines.
2. Pair each with the matching **sector guide** (Banking, Enterprise, Startup, or Government).
3. Fill **Section 10 — How My Experience Maps** with your metrics and stories before mocks.
4. Run the confidence and mock checklists at the end of each playbook.

Do not study all companies at once. Depth on two targets beats shallow coverage of ten.

## Standard Playbook Structure

Every company document follows:

1. Company Overview  
2. Engineering Expectations  
3. Typical Technology Stack  
4. Typical Interview Process  
5. Technical Focus Areas  
6. Leadership Focus  
7. Behavioral Questions (≥30)  
8. System Design Questions  
9. Company Preparation Checklist  
10. How My Experience Maps  
11. Interview Confidence Checklist · Mock Prep · Revision Plan · Estimated Prep Time
