# Microservices with Spring

> Decomposing monoliths and operating Spring Cloud services in production.

---

## Purpose

Show you understand microservices beyond the buzzword — service boundaries, inter-service communication, resilience patterns, and the operational cost that enterprise teams live with daily.

---

## Topics Covered

- [ ] Service decomposition strategies (domain-driven boundaries)
- [ ] Spring Cloud components (Config, Gateway, OpenFeign, Circuit Breaker)
- [ ] Synchronous vs. asynchronous inter-service communication
- [ ] Distributed tracing and correlation across services
- [ ] Saga patterns and distributed transaction alternatives
- [ ] Service discovery and load balancing
- [ ] When NOT to microservice
