# Career Story

> Chronological narrative that connects your roles into a coherent engineering arc.

---

## Purpose

Document the thread that runs through your career — the problems you gravitated toward, the scope you grew into, and the themes that make you a credible Senior/Lead candidate. Interviewers use this to assess trajectory, intentionality, and fit for enterprise engineering organizations.

---

## Topics Covered

- [ ] Career timeline and role progression
- [ ] Key inflection points and scope expansion
- [ ] Domain expertise developed over time
- [ ] Technical themes that repeat across roles
- [ ] Transition moments (IC → lead, team changes, stack shifts)
- [ ] Alignment with target role level

---

## Progress Checklist

- [ ] Timeline drafted with dates and company context
- [ ] Each role has a clear "why I was there" and "what I left behind"
- [ ] Narrative connects to target companies (enterprise Java, regulated industries, scale)
- [ ] Practiced as a 3–5 minute spoken arc
- [ ] Reviewed for gaps or inconsistencies with résumé
