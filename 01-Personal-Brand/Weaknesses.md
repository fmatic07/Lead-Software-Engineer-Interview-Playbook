# Weaknesses

> Honest growth areas framed with self-awareness and action.

---

## Purpose

Answer "what is your greatest weakness?" without disqualifying yourself. Senior panels look for self-awareness, active improvement, and the judgment to know which weaknesses matter at scale — not false humility or disguised strengths.

---

## Topics Covered

- [ ] Genuine growth area (not a hidden strength)
- [ ] Context for why it emerged
- [ ] Concrete steps taken to improve
- [ ] Progress demonstrated over time
- [ ] Why this weakness does not block the target role

---

## Progress Checklist

- [ ] 1–2 weaknesses selected (avoid laundry lists)
- [ ] Each framed with context → action → progress
- [ ] Verified weakness is appropriate for senior level (not fatal flaws)
- [ ] Practiced without oversharing or rambling
- [ ] Mock feedback: sounds authentic, not scripted
