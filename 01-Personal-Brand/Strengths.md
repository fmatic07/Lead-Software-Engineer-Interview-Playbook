# Strengths

> Strengths backed by evidence — not adjectives.

---

## Purpose

Prepare three to five defensible strengths that map to what Senior, Lead, and Architect panels evaluate: technical depth, judgment, communication, and delivery under pressure. Each strength must survive "give me an example" follow-ups.

---

## Topics Covered

- [ ] Technical strengths (architecture, debugging, performance, security)
- [ ] Leadership strengths (mentoring, unblocking, technical direction)
- [ ] Collaboration strengths (stakeholders, cross-team, product partnership)
- [ ] Evidence and metrics for each strength
- [ ] Strength-to-role alignment matrix

---

## Progress Checklist

- [ ] 3–5 strengths identified with STAR examples
- [ ] Each strength has a production story with measurable outcome
- [ ] Avoided generic claims ("hard worker," "team player")
- [ ] Mapped strengths to job descriptions of target companies
- [ ] Practiced concise delivery (30 seconds per strength)
