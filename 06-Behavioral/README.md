# Module 06 — Behavioral Interview Mastery

> An executive communication handbook for Senior → Lead → Tech Lead → Architect behavioral loops — authenticity over memorization.

---

## Audience

Senior Software Engineer · Lead Software Engineer · Technical Lead · Software Architect  
(8+ years assumed)

Prepared for enterprise software companies, banks, startups, and technology organizations — including ING, Globe Telecom, Deltek, Maya, GovTech Singapore, ReciMe, Atlassian, Canva, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google.

This is **not** a generic HR interview guide. It trains engineers to communicate real production experience with leadership presence and credibility.

## How to Use This Module

1. Read **01 Foundations** and **11 Story Library** first — build your cards before grinding questions.
2. Work chapters **02–09** and draft Notes with your real examples.
3. Drill **10 / 10b** aloud; map each question to a story ID from chapter 11.
4. Calibrate every answer for **Senior vs Lead** altitude depending on the target role.

---

## Core Principle

> If you cannot tie the answer to something you shipped, debugged, decided, or led in production, you are not ready to discuss it in a senior behavioral loop.
