# 13 — Interview Evaluation

> Scorecards and rubrics for mock loops and real post-interview debriefs.

---

## Purpose

Replace "I think it went okay" with dimension-level scores, evidence quotes, and a remediation plan. Use alongside `Self-Assessment-Rubric.md`, `Mock-Interview-Log.md`, and `Feedback-Tracker.md`.

---

## How to Use

1. After each mock or real round, score within 30 minutes.
2. Require **evidence** (what you said / failed to say) — not feelings alone.
3. Any dimension ≤2 blocks "ready" status for that round type.
4. Re-score after deliberate practice.

**Scale:** 1 = Fail · 2 = Below bar · 3 = Meet bar · 4 = Strong · 5 = Exceptional for level

## Dimension Rubrics

### Communication

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Rambling, unstructured, interrupts poorly | Clear enough; occasional digressions | Crisp structure; checkpoints; excellent listening |

**Checklist:** Restates questions · Times answers · Invites depth choice · Strong close

---

### Technical (Java / Spring / Stack)

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect mental models | Solid senior APIs/ops basics | Production-grade depth + verification methods |

**Checklist:** Contracts · Failure modes · Observability · Java 21 awareness as relevant

---

### Leadership

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| No people/system stories | Mentoring / ownership examples | Multiplies team; norms; hard conversations |

**Checklist:** Influence without authority · Feedback · Incident command · Hiring bar

---

### Architecture

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Buzzword soup | Coherent service/data design | Constraint-driven; evolution; operability |

**Checklist:** Boundaries · Consistency · Migration · Complexity tax named

---

### Ownership

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Blame / passive "we" | Accountable for tasks | Accountable for outcomes + escalation craft |

**Checklist:** Metrics · Follow-through · Risk acceptance clarity

---

### Problem Solving

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Freezes or guesses | Structured approach | Hypotheses, experiments, prioritization under time |

**Checklist:** Clarify · Decompose · Edges · Validate

---

### Decision Making

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| No criteria | Some tradeoffs | Explicit criteria, reversibility, stakeholders |

**Checklist:** Options · One-way doors · After-action learning

---

### Confidence

| 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Panic, self-sabotage, bluffing | Steady | Composed; honest unknowns; strong presence |

**Checklist:** Pause before answer · No chronic apology · Calm under probe

## Pass Thresholds (Suggested)

| Target level | Ready if |
|--------------|----------|
| Senior | Average ≥ 3.5; no 1s; Technical & Communication ≥ 3 |
| Lead | Average ≥ 4.0; Leadership & Ownership ≥ 4; Architecture ≥ 3 |
| Architect | Architecture & Decision Making ≥ 4; Communication ≥ 4 |

Adjust for company bar (banks/gov often weight risk & communication higher).

## Confidence Checklist (Module Exit)

- [ ] Completed ≥2 full mock loops with logged scorecards
- [ ] All critical dimensions ≥ threshold for target level
- [ ] Weak dimensions have concrete drills (not "study more")
- [ ] Interview day guide rehearsed
- [ ] Feedback incorporated into story bank / tech notes

---

## Calibration Tips

- Score harsher in mocks than you feel — real panels will.
- Compare scores with a peer mock partner weekly.
- Re-watch or re-listen recordings if available.
- Track trend lines in `Feedback-Tracker.md`, not single data points.
