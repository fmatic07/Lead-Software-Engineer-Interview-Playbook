# 11 — Hiring Manager Interview

> Executive-level discussions with your future manager — vision, judgment, and working style fit.

---

## Interview Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 45–60 minutes |
| Interviewer | Engineering Manager / Senior EM / Head of Eng |
| Weight | Very high for offer and team placement |

---

## Purpose

Managers hire for outcomes and working relationship: can they trust you with ambiguity, stakeholders, and growth?

---

## How Interviewers Evaluate

| Theme | Looking for |
|-------|-------------|
| Career vision | Coherent, realistic, aligned to role |
| Leadership | Influence and accountability stories |
| Business thinking | Connects tech to cost/risk/revenue |
| Product thinking | Customer outcomes over output |
| Decision making | Criteria under uncertainty |
| Growth | Coachability + self-direction |
| Ownership | End-to-end responsibility |

---

## Common Mistakes

- Only talking technology with no business frame.
- Saying you want the manager's job in 6 months.
- No questions for them (signal of low interest).
- Blaming prior managers extensively.
- Vague growth areas.

---

## Excellent Communication Techniques

Mirror their language on team goals. Bring questions that show you understand constraints (legacy, compliance, hiring freeze, deadlines).

---

## Confidence Tips

Research the manager's public posts/talks if any; prepare 3 thoughtful questions.

---

## Ideal Answer Framework

**Goal alignment → Evidence story → Working preferences → Mutual fit ask**

---

## Topic Scripts

### Career Vision

**Interviewer:** Where do you want to be in 3 years?

**Candidate:** Deepening technical leadership toward [Staff/Architect/Lead of platform] — still hands-on enough to stay honest, with larger design and mentoring scope. I'm not optimizing for pure people management unless the org needs it; I want leverage through systems and people growth.

**Follow-up:** Why not EM track?

**Strong:** Respect EM craft; prefer IC leadership today; open if mutual fit later.

---

### Leadership

**Interviewer:** How do you lead seniors who don't report to you?

**Candidate:** Shared goals, written proposals, data, and earned trust. Escalate disagreements with options. Celebrate their wins publicly; feedback privately.

---

### Business Thinking

**Interviewer:** Engineering wants a rewrite; sales needs features. Your stance?

**Candidate:** Quantify rewrite risk/time vs incremental strangler enabling sales outcomes. Propose thin slices that reduce risk *and* unlock revenue. Avoid moralizing "correct architecture."

---

### Product Thinking

**Interviewer:** Tell me about a time you changed a product decision.

**Candidate:** STAR with user evidence/metrics; partnership tone with PM; outcome.

---

### Decision Making

**Interviewer:** Describe a high-stakes technical decision you made with incomplete data.

**Candidate:** Options, criteria, what was unknown, reversible vs one-way door, how you validated after, what you'd redo.

---

### Growth

**Interviewer:** What do you need from a manager to be successful?

**Candidate:** Clear priorities, fast feedback, protection for focus time, support in stakeholder conflicts, honesty on promotion criteria. I bring proactive status and no surprises.

---

### Ownership

**Interviewer:** What does ownership mean on your current team?

**Candidate:** Include operability, quality bar, mentoring, and customer impact — not just merging PRs.

---

## Additional HM Prompts

1. How do you handle competing priorities from two PMs?
2. What's the hardest feedback you've received?
3. How do you onboard into a messy codebase in 30 days?
4. Tell me about a time you missed — how did you communicate?
5. How do you keep quality high under deadline?
6. What management styles fail with you?
7. How do you measure your own performance?
8. Why this team vs sister team?
9. Compensation flexibility vs scope preference?
10. What would make you leave a team in year one?

---

## Full Script — Mutual Fit

**Interviewer:** What questions do you have for me?

**Candidate (strong set):**
1. What would make someone extraordinary in this role in year one?
2. What is currently under-invested on the team (platform, quality, hiring)?
3. How do you run performance and promotion decisions?
4. What is the hardest stakeholder dynamic I should know?
5. How does the team handle on-call and burnout risk?

**Evaluation Notes:** Quality of questions often decides "I'd enjoy managing this person."

---

## Evaluation Rubric

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| Vision | Confused | Plausible | Clear + aligned |
| Business lens | Absent | Occasional | Natural |
| Ownership | Narrow | Solid | End-to-end |
| Coachability | Defensive | Open | Actively reflective |
| Manager fit | Red flags | Neutral | High trust signal |

- [ ] One decision-under-ambiguity story
- [ ] Growth needs articulated without blaming
- [ ] 5 strong questions prepared
