# 01 — HR Interview

> Screen for clarity, motivation, culture fit, and compensation alignment — before technical panels invest time.

---

## Interview Overview

HR / Talent Acquisition rounds filter logistics and narrative coherence. They rarely deep-dive Spring transactions, but they do detect rehearsed emptiness, salary misalignment, and red flags about leaving poorly.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 20–40 minutes |
| Format | Conversational; sometimes structured scorecard |
| Decision weight | Gate: pass/fail or "proceed with caution" |
| Typical interviewers | Recruiter, TA partner, occasionally People Ops |

---

## Purpose

- Confirm you can explain your career without chaos.
- Validate motivation for *this* company and role.
- Surface notice period, location, visa, and compensation bands early.
- Spot collaboration and integrity signals.

---

## How Interviewers Evaluate Candidates

| Signal | Strong | Weak |
|--------|--------|------|
| Narrative | Crisp 2-min arc with ownership | Chronological résumé dump |
| Motivation | Specific to company problem/domain | "Looking for new opportunities" |
| Self-awareness | Honest growth areas with mitigation | "I work too hard" clichés |
| Compensation | Research-backed range + flexibility language | Ultimatum or "whatever you offer" |
| Professionalism | Warm, concise, on time | Late, vague, blame-heavy exits |

---

## Common Mistakes

- Speaking negatively about current manager or company for more than one calibrated sentence.
- No salary range when asked (or inventing a number mid-call).
- Cannot explain why *this* company vs competitors.
- Oversharing personal drama.
- Treating HR as "not important" — they influence process speed and offer framing.

---

## Excellent Communication Techniques

- Answer in 60–120 seconds; invite follow-up.
- Use past → present → future for Tell Me About Yourself.
- Frame exits as pull factors (mission, scope, craft) more than push factors.
- Mirror their language on level (Senior / Lead / Architect).

---

## Confidence Tips

- Treat HR as the first stakeholder you lead with clarity.
- Have notice period, expected CTC/OTE band, and earliest start date written down.
- Smile with voice; energy transfers on phone screens.

---

## Ideal Answer Framework (HR)

**Context (1 sentence) → Contribution (1–2) → Motivation (1) → Fit ask (optional)**

## Question Bank (50+)

Practice aloud. For starred items (★), full scripts follow the bank.

### Opening & Narrative
1. ★ Tell me about yourself.
2. Walk me through your résumé in 3 minutes.
3. What is your current role and scope?
4. What technologies do you use day to day?
5. How large is your team and what is your influence beyond it?

### Motivation & Company Fit
6. ★ Why are you leaving your current role?
7. ★ Why do you want to join our company?
8. Why this role (Senior / Lead / Architect)?
9. What do you know about our products / domain?
10. What attracted you to our engineering culture?
11. Why enterprise / banking / telco / startup / consulting / government (as applicable)?
12. Where else are you interviewing?
13. What would make you decline an offer from us?

### Compensation & Logistics
14. ★ What are your salary expectations?
15. What is your current compensation (base / bonus / equity)?
16. Are you open to our band if below your ask?
17. What is your notice period?
18. Are you open to hybrid / on-site / relocation?
19. Do you have any constraints we should know about?
20. When can you start?

### Achievement & Impact
21. ★ What is your greatest professional achievement?
22. Describe a project you are most proud of.
23. How do you measure your impact?
24. Tell me about a time you saved cost or reduced risk.
25. What production incident did you help resolve?

### Failure & Growth
26. ★ Tell me about a failure.
27. What is your greatest weakness?
28. Describe critical feedback you received and how you changed.
29. When did you underperform — what did you do?
30. What skill are you actively improving?

### Career Goals
31. ★ Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
32. Do you want to stay technical or move to management?
33. What does "Lead" mean to you?
34. How do you want to grow in the first year here?
35. What kind of mentor or manager do you work best with?

### Collaboration & Culture
36. How do you handle disagreement with a manager?
37. Describe working with difficult stakeholders.
38. Prefer IC deep work or cross-team coordination?
39. How do you communicate bad news?
40. What kind of team environment helps you do your best work?

### Work Style
41. How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?
42. Remote collaboration habits?
43. How do you document decisions?
44. On-call experience and comfort?
45. How many hours do you typically work — and how do you protect sustainability?

### Integrity & Edge Cases
46. Have you ever missed a deadline? What happened?
47. Conflict with a peer — resolution?
48. Ever pushed back on an unethical or unsafe request?
49. Why was there a gap in your employment? (if any)
50. Why short tenure at Company X? (if any)
51. Can we contact your current employer for references?
52. Anything else we should know before proceeding?

## Full Scripts (Representative)

### Q1 — Tell me about yourself ★

**What interviewer is evaluating:** Structure, seniority signal, relevance to role.

**Ideal framework:** Past (brief) → Present (depth) → Future (why here).

**Interviewer:** Tell me about yourself.

**Candidate (Senior):** I'm a Senior Software Engineer with about [N] years in Java and Spring Boot, mostly in [domain]. Recently I've owned [service/domain], including [one concrete outcome with metric]. Before that I [earlier relevant arc in one sentence]. I'm looking for a Lead-track environment where I can deepen system design and mentor while still shipping — which is why your [product/team] stood out.

**Candidate (Lead):** I'm a Lead Software Engineer focused on backend platforms in Java/Spring. I currently lead delivery across [scope], partner with product on sequencing, and drive standards around APIs, observability, and incident response. A recent example: we [migration/reliability win]. I'm exploring roles where technical leadership and architecture influence are first-class — aligned with how your team [specific public signal].

**Follow-up:** What would your manager say is your strongest contribution this year?

**Expected Senior Answer:** Name one owned outcome with metric and collaboration boundary.

**Expected Lead Answer:** Name team-level outcome + how you unblocked others.

**Evaluation Notes:** Fail if >4 minutes or pure tool laundry list.

---

### Q6 — Why are you leaving? ★

**Interviewer:** Why are you leaving your current company?

**Candidate:** I've learned a lot in [domain], especially [skill]. The work is stabilizing toward [maintenance/narrow scope], and I'm looking for [broader ownership / scale / regulated domain / architecture influence]. It's a pull toward growth, not a dramatic exit — I've given strong notice planning and left relationships intact.

**Follow-up:** What have you tried internally before looking outside?

**Excellent Senior:** Mentored, took stretch designs, asked for scope — hit org ceiling.

**Excellent Lead:** Proposed platform initiatives; timing/headcount constrained; seeking environment that funds that work.

**Common mistakes:** Manager bashing; "toxic" without professionalism; salary as only reason on first mention.

**Evaluation rubric:** Honesty 1–5, professionalism 1–5, forward-looking 1–5.

---

### Q7 — Why our company? ★

**Interviewer:** Why us?

**Candidate:** Three reasons: (1) domain — [payments/telco/gov] matches problems I've shipped; (2) engineering bar — [stack or public eng blog/tech talk]; (3) role shape — [Lead/Architect] influence on [platform]. I also noticed [recent product or initiative], which maps to my experience with [related system].

**Follow-up:** What concerns do you have about joining?

**Strong close:** Name one thoughtful risk (legacy, pace) and how you'd validate in onboarding — shows maturity.

---

### Q14 — Salary expectations ★

**Interviewer:** What are your salary expectations?

**Candidate:** Based on market data for [level] in [location] and the scope we discussed, I'm targeting [range]. I'm flexible for the right total package — base, bonus, equity/benefits, and growth scope. Happy to align within your band if the role matches.

**Follow-up:** What is your current CTC?

**Technique:** Answer factually if comfortable; or "I'm focused on market for the new scope; happy to share current later in process." Know local norms (banks often expect current CTC).

**Confidence checklist:** Range researched; floor known; non-salary levers listed.

---

### Q21 — Greatest achievement ★

**Interviewer:** Greatest professional achievement?

**Candidate (STAR):** Situation: [risk/scale]. Task: [ownership]. Action: [2–3 concrete moves]. Result: [metric + qualitative]. Lesson: [reusable principle].

**Senior lens:** Deep ownership of a hard technical outcome.  
**Lead lens:** Outcome that raised team capability or reduced org risk.

---

### Q26 — Failure ★

**Interviewer:** Tell me about a failure.

**Candidate:** I [decision]. Impact was [honest]. I owned it by [mitigation]. Systemically we [process/test/alert change]. I now [habit].

**Trap:** Fake failures ("cared too much"). Real failure with learning scores higher.

---

### Q31 — Career goals ★

**Interviewer:** Where do you see yourself in five years?

**Candidate:** Still deeply technical — [Staff/Architect/Lead] path — influencing design quality and mentoring. I want to be the person trusted on ambiguous platform bets, not necessarily people-manager unless the org needs it. In year one here I'd aim to [own domain, stabilize X, raise Y bar].

## Evaluation Rubric (HR Round)

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| Narrative clarity | Scattered | Adequate | Memorable, role-aligned |
| Motivation | Generic | Plausible | Specific and researched |
| Professionalism | Blame / chaos | Neutral | Diplomatic and precise |
| Logistics readiness | Unknown | Partial | Clear dates, constraints, range |
| Culture signal | Self only | Cooperative | Stakeholder-aware |

**Pass threshold:** No 1s; average ≥3.5.

## Confidence Checklist

- [ ] Company-specific "why us" with 3 bullets
- [ ] Compensation range written and rehearsed
- [ ] Notice / start date / location constraints clear
- [ ] One achievement + one failure STAR ready
- [ ] 3 questions prepared for recruiter
