# Mock Interview Log

> Record of mock interview sessions, questions asked, and performance notes.

---

## Purpose

Track every mock interview session — format, questions, your answers, and areas for improvement. Treat mocks as production deployments: iterate until confidence is earned, not assumed.

---

## Topics Covered

- [ ] Session metadata (date, format, interviewer, duration)
- [ ] Questions asked by category (technical, system design, behavioral, leadership)
- [ ] Self-assessment per question (strong / adequate / weak)
- [ ] Follow-up questions that exposed gaps
- [ ] Action items before next session
- [ ] Comparison across sessions (progress tracking)

---

## Progress Checklist

- [ ] Completed mock session #1 (full loop or focused)
- [ ] Completed mock session #2 (different focus area)
- [ ] Logged all questions and self-assessments
- [ ] Addressed top 3 gaps between sessions
- [ ] Final confidence check before real interviews
