# Career Reflection

> Templates to turn experience into judgment — the advantage senior panels actually hire.

---

## Purpose

Provide reusable templates for career journaling, lessons learned, interview retrospectives, offer comparison, quarterly reviews, and annual engineering growth plans. Use after each loop and each quarter.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Reflection without decisions is diary; pair every insight with an action.
- Interview retrospectives compound faster than more LeetCode hours alone.
- Quarterly reviews prevent accidental careers.
- Annual plans connect learning to track (Staff / EM / Architect).
- Keep templates light enough you’ll actually fill them.

## Career Journal

```text

## Context

3.

## Decisions I made
```

### Cadence

10–15 minutes weekly on Friday. Monthly: skim and tag patterns (`#influence`, `#debt`, `#manager`).

## Lessons Learned

### Template

```text
# Lesson – [Title]

Date:
Domain: (technical / leadership / career / communication)

## What happened
## What I expected
## What was true
## System causes (not just personal)
## Rule I’ll use next time
## How I’ll practice the rule
## Share with? (mentor / team / private)
```

### Real-world example

**Title:** Don’t propose strangler migrations without ops capacity  
**Rule:** Every migration RFC includes on-call load estimate and rollback owner.  
**Practice:** Add section to RFC template; reject incomplete RFCs in review.

## Interview Retrospective

### Template

```text
# Interview Retro – [Company] – [Role] – [Date]

## Loop map
Rounds / interviewers / topics:

## What went well
## Where I struggled
## Questions that surprised me
## Stories that landed / failed
## Technical gaps to study
## Process / communication gaps
## Signal on company (culture, manager, bar)

## Score (1–5)
Technical depth:
System design:
Leadership/behavioral:
Rapport:
My interest post-loop:

## Actions (due dates)
- [ ]
- [ ]

## Reuse in story bank?
```

### Decision framework

| Pattern across 2+ retros | Action |
|--------------------------|--------|
| Weak metrics in stories | Add numbers this week |
| Design: failure modes thin | Drill outage scenarios |
| Nervous early screens | Script opener; mock twice |
| Company interest drops post-HM | Trust that signal |

## Decision Frameworks

### When to open the job market

Open explore mode if quarterly review shows 2+ consecutive quarters of flat learning, blocked scope, or >15% comp gap without path — aligned with Career Planning stay/leave rules.

### When to stay and invest

Manager is a multiplier, problem difficulty rising, promo path evidenced, health stable.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Writing retros only after failures (also capture wins).
- Vague actions (“get better at system design”) without drills.
- Annual plans that ignore family/health constraints.
- Never revisiting the capability thesis.
